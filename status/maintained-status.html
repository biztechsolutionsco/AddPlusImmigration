TEMPORARY RESIDENT STATUS

What Is Maintained Status?

Maintained status is the legal ability of certain temporary residents to remain in Canada after their existing status expires while IRCC processes an eligible application submitted before that expiry.

It was previously commonly referred to as "implied status."

Maintained status can apply to visitors, workers and students, but the activities a person may continue while waiting for a decision depend on their previous status and the application they submitted.

Applying before expiry is therefore critical. An application submitted after status has already expired generally does not create maintained status.

At a Glance

HOW IT WORKS

Maintained Status Begins With a Timely Application

To benefit from maintained status, IRCC must generally receive the appropriate application before the person's current temporary status expires.

Once the existing status expires, the person may continue to remain legally in Canada while IRCC processes the eligible application.

The application type matters. Extending worker status, extending student status and changing to visitor status can lead to different rights while the application is pending.

Before Expiry Is the Key

A late application generally cannot create maintained status retroactively.

WHO MAY QUALIFY?

Visitors, Workers and Students

Maintained status can apply to different temporary resident categories, but the consequences are not identical.

01

Visitors

A visitor who applies to extend their authorized stay before expiry may generally remain in Canada as a temporary resident while IRCC processes the application.

02

Workers

A worker who applies to extend or change their work permit before expiry may generally remain in Canada while IRCC processes the application.

03

Students

A student who applies to extend or change their study permit before expiry may generally remain legally in Canada while waiting for IRCC's decision.

04

Changing Temporary Status

A worker or student may apply to remain in Canada as a visitor, but this does not necessarily preserve their previous work or study authorization.

05

Permit Conditions Matter

The conditions attached to the previous permit can determine what activities remain authorized while waiting.

06

Special Categories

Some immigration documents or programs have special rules, so maintained status should not be assumed in every temporary immigration situation.

WORKERS

Can You Keep Working on Maintained Status?

If you applied to extend or change the conditions of your work permit before it expired, you may generally continue working until IRCC makes a decision, provided the applicable requirements are met.

You generally must remain in Canada and continue working under the same conditions as your original work permit.

For example, if you hold an employer-specific work permit, maintained status does not normally allow you to begin working for another employer simply because a new application is pending.

Same Conditions Generally Continue

Maintained status preserves existing work authorization in qualifying cases. It does not automatically create broader employment rights.

CHANGING STATUS

Applying as a Visitor Does Not Preserve Work Authorization

Maintaining legal temporary residence in Canada does not always mean maintaining permission to work.

W

Worker → Work Permit

If an eligible worker applies for another work permit before expiry, they may generally continue working under the applicable existing conditions.

V

Worker → Visitor

If the worker applies to remain as a visitor, they may remain in Canada while the visitor application is pending, but must generally stop working when the original work permit expires.

S

Worker → Student

If the worker applies for a study permit instead, they generally must stop working when the original work permit expires and cannot begin studies until authorized.

!

Status and Activity Are Separate

A person may remain legally in Canada while still being prohibited from working or studying.

STUDENTS

Can You Keep Studying on Maintained Status?

If you apply to extend or change the conditions of your study permit before it expires, you may generally remain legally in Canada while IRCC processes your application.

In qualifying circumstances, a student who filed the appropriate extension before expiry can generally continue studying under the conditions of the existing permit while waiting.

If the study permit expired before the application was submitted, maintained status does not apply and the student generally must stop studying.

File Before Your Study Permit Expires

The difference between a timely extension and a late application can determine whether you may continue studying while waiting.

VISITORS

Can a Visitor Remain in Canada While an Extension Is Processing?

A visitor who applies to extend their stay before their current visitor status expires may generally remain legally in Canada while IRCC decides the application.

The person continues to have temporary resident status, but visitor status does not by itself authorize employment or studies that require a permit.

If the extension is approved, IRCC may issue a visitor record showing the new date by which the person must leave Canada.

Visitor Status Is Not Work Authorization

Staying legally in Canada while a visitor extension is pending does not give the person permission to work.

APPLICATION DEADLINE

IRCC Must Receive the Application Before Expiry

Do not assume that starting an online application is enough. It must actually be submitted on time.

01

Online Applications

For online work permit extension applications, IRCC currently uses Coordinated Universal Time when determining whether an application was received before expiry.

02

Local Time Can Differ

Because IRCC's online system uses UTC, the deadline may arrive earlier than midnight in your local Canadian time zone.

03

Submit Early

Waiting until the expiry date creates unnecessary risk from technical problems, payment issues or missing documents.

04

Keep Proof of Submission

Save confirmation showing when the application was successfully submitted.

05

Check Permit Expiry

Use the actual expiry date on your immigration document rather than relying on assumptions about when status ends.

06

Earlier Is Better

For work, study and visitor extensions, IRCC generally recommends applying before the last month of validity whenever possible.

TRAVEL OUTSIDE CANADA

Leaving Canada Can Affect Your Maintained Status Rights

Maintained status is particularly important for people remaining physically in Canada while IRCC processes an application.

A worker on maintained status who leaves Canada may lose the ability to continue working when they return and may have to wait until the new work permit is approved.

A study or work permit is also not itself a travel document. Travellers must still satisfy the applicable passport, TRV or eTA requirements when seeking re-entry.

Admission to Canada is ultimately determined at the border.

OTHER DOCUMENTS

What About Your SIN, Health Card or Driver's Licence?

Maintained status can allow a qualifying temporary resident to remain legally in Canada, but that does not automatically mean every other government document can be renewed while the immigration application is pending.

IRCC currently notes that people on maintained status may be unable to renew certain provincial or territorial documents, such as a driver's licence or health card, until they receive their new immigration document.

Eligible workers may continue working with an expired SIN while their qualifying work permit extension is being processed, but the SIN expiry generally cannot be updated until the new work permit is issued.

Immigration Status and Document Renewal Are Different

Consider renewing time-sensitive provincial documents before your current immigration permit expires where possible.

AFTER THE DECISION

Maintained Status Does Not Continue Indefinitely

Maintained status exists while IRCC is processing the qualifying application.

Once IRCC makes a decision, the person's immigration situation changes according to the result of that application.

If the application is approved, the new immigration document and its conditions apply.

If the application is refused, the person should immediately review whether restoration, another immigration option or departure from Canada is required.

Refused Application?

Do not assume you can continue working, studying or remaining in Canada under the previous maintained-status conditions after refusal.

MAINTAINED STATUS OR RESTORATION?

The Difference Is Usually Whether You Applied Before Expiry

These concepts are related, but they describe very different immigration situations.

M

Maintained Status

Generally arises when an eligible extension or change application was submitted before temporary status expired.

R

Restoration

May be relevant after temporary resident status has already been lost, subject to separate eligibility and timing requirements.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Maintained Status Does Not Mean "Everything Continues Automatically"

Maintained status is often misunderstood. It can allow a person to remain legally in Canada while an application is pending, but it does not necessarily preserve every activity they were previously authorized to perform.

Whether you may work or study depends on your previous immigration status, the application you submitted, the conditions of your previous permit and whether you remain in Canada.

Travel, changing immigration categories, changing employers or filing after expiry can significantly affect the analysis.
