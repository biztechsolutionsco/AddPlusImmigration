FEDERAL SKILLED WORKER PROGRAM

What Is the Federal Skilled Worker Program?

The Federal Skilled Worker Program, commonly known as FSWP, is one of Canada's federal economic immigration programs managed through Express Entry.

It is intended for skilled workers who have qualifying work experience and meet minimum requirements relating to language ability, education and skilled employment.

The program can be particularly relevant for applicants whose qualifying work experience was gained outside Canada.

Applicants must first meet the program's minimum requirements and then obtain at least 67 points under the Federal Skilled Worker selection grid before they may qualify to enter the Express Entry pool.

At a Glance

Work Experience At least 1 continuous year within the last 10 years
Eligible Work NOC TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3
Language Minimum CLB/NCLC 7
Education At least secondary education
Selection Grid Minimum 67 out of 100 points

ELIGIBILITY

Minimum FSWP Requirements

Before the 67-point selection grid is considered, applicants must first satisfy the program's minimum requirements.

01

Skilled Work Experience

You generally need at least one year of continuous qualifying skilled work experience, equal to at least 1,560 hours.

02

Eligible NOC

The work experience used as your primary occupation must be classified in NOC TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3.

03

Recent Experience

Your qualifying work experience must generally have been obtained within the ten years before you apply.

04

Language Ability

You must take an approved English or French test and achieve at least CLB or NCLC 7 in all four language abilities.

05

Education

You must have at least a Canadian secondary educational credential or a qualifying foreign credential supported by an Educational Credential Assessment.

06

67-Point Threshold

After meeting the minimum requirements, you must score at least 67 out of 100 points under the FSWP selection factor system.

WORK EXPERIENCE

Understanding Qualifying Work Experience

Your work history must satisfy several requirements before it can be used for Federal Skilled Worker Program eligibility.

✓

TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3

Your primary occupation must fall within an eligible skilled NOC category.

✓

At Least 1,560 Hours

You generally need at least one year of continuous qualifying work, equivalent to 1,560 hours.

✓

Paid Employment

The qualifying experience must be paid employment. Volunteer work and unpaid internships do not count.

✓

Correct NOC Duties

Your actual duties must correspond with the NOC lead statement and most of the main duties for your claimed occupation.

✓

Canadian or Foreign Experience

Qualifying work experience may have been obtained in Canada or outside Canada, provided the applicable requirements are met.

✓

Student Work May Count

Unlike the Canadian Experience Class, qualifying work completed while studying may count under FSWP if it was paid, continuous and meets all other program requirements.

SELECTION FACTORS

The 67-Point FSWP Selection Grid

Candidates who meet the minimum program requirements are assessed using six selection factors for a maximum total of 100 points.

UP TO 28 POINTS

Language Skills

Points are awarded for proficiency in Canada's official languages, based on approved language test results.

UP TO 25 POINTS

Education

Points depend on your completed level of education and, for foreign credentials, the applicable Educational Credential Assessment.

UP TO 15 POINTS

Work Experience

Additional years of eligible skilled work experience can increase your selection-factor score.

UP TO 12 POINTS

Age

Age is assessed according to the program's selection grid, with the number of points varying by age.

UP TO 10 POINTS

Arranged Employment

A qualifying Canadian job offer may contribute selection-factor points when the applicable requirements are satisfied.

UP TO 10 POINTS

Adaptability

Factors involving you or an accompanying spouse or partner may provide additional adaptability points.

AN IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

FSWP Points and CRS Points Are Not the Same

One of the most common areas of confusion in Express Entry is the difference between the Federal Skilled Worker selection grid and the Comprehensive Ranking System.

The 67-point FSWP grid is used to determine whether you meet the Federal Skilled Worker Program's selection threshold.

Once you qualify and enter the Express Entry pool, the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) is used to rank your profile against other candidates.

It is therefore possible to meet the 67-point threshold but still have a CRS score that is not high enough to receive an invitation in a particular round.

67 Points ≠ CRS Score

These are two separate scoring systems used at different stages of the Federal Skilled Worker and Express Entry process.

LANGUAGE & EDUCATION

Two Core Eligibility Requirements

Both language ability and education are fundamental to Federal Skilled Worker Program eligibility.

7

Minimum Language Level

Applicants must currently achieve at least CLB 7 in English or NCLC 7 in French in speaking, listening, reading and writing.

✓

Approved Language Test

Language ability must be demonstrated through an approved English or French language examination.

✓

Canadian Education

A qualifying Canadian secondary or post-secondary certificate, diploma or degree may satisfy the education requirement.

✓

Foreign Education

Applicants relying on foreign education generally require a completed credential and an Educational Credential Assessment for immigration purposes.

SETTLEMENT FUNDS

Proof of Funds May Be Required

Federal Skilled Worker applicants generally need to demonstrate that they have sufficient funds to support themselves and their family after arriving in Canada.

The required amount depends on family size and is updated periodically by IRCC.

An exemption may apply if you are currently legally authorized to work in Canada and have a valid job offer from a Canadian employer.

Avoid Using an Outdated Funds Amount

IRCC updates settlement-fund requirements. Always verify the current amount applicable to your family size before submitting an application.

APPLICATION PROCESS

How the FSWP Process Works

Federal Skilled Worker applications are managed through Canada's Express Entry system.

1

Confirm Minimum Eligibility

Review your skilled work experience, language results and education.

2

Calculate FSWP Points

Determine whether you meet the required 67-point selection threshold.

3

Create Express Entry Profile

If eligible, create your Express Entry profile and enter the candidate pool.

4

Receive an Invitation

Selected candidates may receive an Invitation to Apply for permanent residence.

5

Apply for PR

If invited, submit the permanent residence application and supporting documentation within the applicable deadline.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Your NOC and Employment Evidence Matter

A job title alone does not determine whether work experience qualifies under the Federal Skilled Worker Program.

Your actual employment duties should correspond with the lead statement and most of the main duties of the NOC occupation you are claiming.

Employment records should also support the period of employment, hours worked, position, compensation and duties claimed in your application.
