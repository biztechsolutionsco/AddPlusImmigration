ABOUT ADDPLUS

Who We Are

AddPlus Immigration Solutions is a Canada-based immigration consulting firm dedicated to providing high-quality, ethical and client-centred immigration services.

We specialize in study permits, work permits, permanent residence pathways, and immigration matters before Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) and the Immigration and Refugee Board of Canada (IRB).

What sets AddPlus apart is not only our technical expertise, but our deep understanding of the newcomer journey.

PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE

Our Team & Professional Credentials

Experienced immigration professionals committed to providing clear, ethical and reliable guidance.

All of our immigration counsellors are licensed and authorized by IRCC, with IRB representation experience where applicable.

Our team brings over 20 years of combined experience supporting individuals and families with studying, working and immigrating to Canada.

We have advised international students, skilled workers, families and newcomers from diverse backgrounds, guiding them through complex immigration processes with clarity, professionalism and care.

OUR STORY

Our Founders' Background

The founders of AddPlus have spent many years working directly with newcomers in non-profit and community-based organizations across Canada.

Prior to establishing AddPlus, they supported immigrants and refugees through settlement services, social programs and frontline community work.

This background allows us to see beyond applications and paperwork. We understand the real challenges newcomers face, from navigating unfamiliar systems to building a future in a new country.

We bring that perspective into every case we handle.
