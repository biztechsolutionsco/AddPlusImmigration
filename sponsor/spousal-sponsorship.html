FAMILY SPONSORSHIP

What Is Spousal Sponsorship?

Canada's family sponsorship program allows eligible Canadian citizens and permanent residents to sponsor their spouse to become a permanent resident of Canada.

To qualify as a spouse, you and your partner must be legally married and the relationship must be genuine rather than entered into primarily for immigration purposes.

A spousal sponsorship application includes both the sponsor's application and the sponsored spouse's application for permanent residence.

IRCC assesses both the sponsor's eligibility and whether the sponsored person meets the applicable relationship, admissibility and permanent residence requirements.

SPONSOR ELIGIBILITY

Who Can Sponsor a Spouse?

The person sponsoring a spouse must meet Canada's sponsorship requirements.

01

At Least 18 Years Old

The sponsor must be at least 18 years of age when applying.

02

Eligible Status in Canada

The sponsor must generally be a Canadian citizen, a Canadian permanent resident, or a person registered in Canada under the Indian Act.

03

Residence Requirements

Permanent residents generally must live in Canada to sponsor. Canadian citizens living abroad may be able to sponsor if they demonstrate plans to return to Canada when the sponsored person becomes a permanent resident.

04

Financial Undertaking

The sponsor must sign an undertaking agreeing to provide financial support for the sponsored spouse for the required period.

05

Sponsorship Eligibility

Certain circumstances, including some previous sponsorships, social assistance, financial defaults or criminal convictions, can affect eligibility to sponsor.

06

Genuine Sponsorship

The application must involve a genuine relationship and must comply with Canada's immigration laws and sponsorship requirements.

WHO CAN BE SPONSORED

Requirements for the Sponsored Spouse

The spouse being sponsored must also meet eligibility and admissibility requirements.

✓

Legally Married

The sponsored person must be legally married to the sponsor.

✓

At Least 18 Years Old

The sponsored spouse must currently be at least 18 years of age.

✓

Genuine Relationship

The marriage must represent a genuine relationship and not exist primarily for the purpose of obtaining immigration status.

✓

Admissibility

The sponsored spouse must meet Canada's applicable immigration admissibility requirements.

SUPPORTING EVIDENCE

Showing That Your Relationship Is Genuine

A marriage certificate alone may not tell the complete story of a relationship. Supporting evidence helps IRCC understand your relationship and life together.

01

Relationship History

Evidence can help explain how you met, how the relationship developed and important milestones in your life together.

02

Communication

Communication records may help demonstrate an ongoing relationship, particularly where spouses have spent periods living apart.

03

Time Together

Travel records, photographs and other evidence can help document time spent together and shared experiences.

04

Shared Residence

Where applicable, documents showing that spouses live or have lived together may support the application.

05

Financial Connection

Shared financial responsibilities, accounts, insurance or other arrangements may help demonstrate how the couple manages their life together.

06

Family & Social Recognition

Evidence showing that family members and friends know about and recognize the relationship may also be relevant.

APPLICATION SITUATION

Your Spouse May Be in Canada or Outside Canada

Spousal sponsorship cases can involve spouses who are already living together in Canada or spouses who are living in different countries.

The appropriate application strategy can depend on where the sponsored spouse lives, their immigration status, travel needs and other circumstances.

A spouse or partner being sponsored while living in Canada may also be eligible to apply for an open work permit if the applicable requirements are met.

Spouse Already in Canada?

Immigration status and work authorization should be considered separately from the permanent residence sponsorship application.

SPONSOR RESPONSIBILITY

Understanding the Sponsorship Undertaking

Sponsorship creates an ongoing financial responsibility for the sponsor.

3

Three-Year Undertaking

Outside Quebec, the undertaking for a sponsored spouse currently lasts for three years beginning when the spouse becomes a permanent resident.

✓

Basic Financial Support

The sponsor agrees to provide the sponsored person with necessary financial support during the undertaking period.

✓

Social Assistance Responsibility

If the sponsored spouse receives certain social assistance during the undertaking period, the sponsor may be required to repay that amount.

✓

Relationship Changes Do Not End It

Once the sponsored spouse becomes a permanent resident, separation or divorce does not automatically cancel or shorten the sponsorship undertaking.

FINANCIAL ELIGIBILITY

Is There a Minimum Income Requirement?

In most spousal sponsorship cases, there is currently no minimum income requirement.

However, a sponsor must still agree to support the sponsored spouse and must not be in certain situations that make them ineligible to sponsor.

An income requirement can apply in limited circumstances involving dependent children who themselves have dependent children.

No Standard Minimum Income in Most Cases

Do not assume that being unemployed or having a modest income automatically makes someone ineligible to sponsor a spouse. The complete sponsorship circumstances must be reviewed.

APPLICATION PROCESS

How Spousal Sponsorship Works

A spousal sponsorship application involves both a sponsorship assessment and a permanent residence application.

1

Confirm Eligibility

Review whether the sponsor and spouse meet the applicable sponsorship requirements.

2

Prepare Relationship Evidence

Gather documents that demonstrate the marriage and the genuine nature of the relationship.

3

Prepare Both Applications

Complete the sponsorship application and the sponsored spouse's permanent residence application.

4

Submit to IRCC

The principal applicant submits the sponsorship and permanent residence applications together through the applicable process.

5

Complete Processing

IRCC may request biometrics, medical examinations, background information, additional documents or other steps before making a decision.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Every Relationship Has Its Own Story

Spousal sponsorship applications are not only about completing forms. IRCC must also be satisfied that the relationship meets Canada's immigration requirements.

Couples may have different circumstances, including long-distance relationships, previous marriages, cultural differences, short courtships, periods of separation or limited shared financial documentation.

These circumstances do not necessarily prevent sponsorship, but the application should explain the relationship clearly and provide appropriate supporting evidence.
