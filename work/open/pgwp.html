POST-GRADUATION WORK PERMIT

Work in Canada After Graduation

The Post-Graduation Work Permit, commonly known as a PGWP, is an open work permit available to eligible graduates of certain Canadian educational programs.

A PGWP can allow graduates to work for most employers in Canada without requiring a specific job offer or Labour Market Impact Assessment.

Canadian work experience gained after graduation may also help some graduates qualify for permanent residence programs such as the Canadian Experience Class or Provincial Nominee Programs.

However, graduating from a Canadian school does not automatically make a student eligible for a PGWP. The institution, program, study history, language results and, in some cases, field of study can all affect eligibility.

OPEN WORK PERMIT

What Can You Do With a PGWP?

A PGWP provides eligible graduates with significant flexibility in the Canadian labour market.

01

Work for Most Employers

A PGWP is generally an open work permit, which means it is not tied to one specific Canadian employer.

02

No Job Offer Required

You do not generally need to secure a Canadian job offer before applying for a PGWP.

03

Change Employers

PGWP holders can generally change employers without applying for a new employer-specific work permit.

04

Build Canadian Experience

Eligible Canadian work experience may support future applications under certain economic immigration programs.

05

Work Anywhere in Canada

A PGWP is generally not restricted to one province or territory unless specific conditions appear on the permit.

06

Temporary Status

A PGWP provides temporary work authorization. It does not automatically provide permanent resident status.

ELIGIBILITY

General PGWP Eligibility Requirements

Applicants must meet both general eligibility requirements and any additional requirements that apply to their level and field of study.

01

PGWP-Eligible School and Program

You must generally complete an eligible program at a designated learning institution that offers PGWP-eligible programs.

02

Minimum Program Length

Your program must generally be at least eight months in length, subject to the applicable rules for your credential and province.

03

Full-Time Studies

You generally must have maintained full-time student status during each semester of your program.

04

Final Semester Exception

Studying part-time during your final academic semester may be permitted if you otherwise meet the PGWP requirements.

05

Complete Your Program

Your school must confirm that you have completed the requirements for your eligible program of study.

06

Apply Within the Deadline

You generally have up to 180 days after receiving confirmation that you completed your program to apply for a PGWP.

SCHOOL & PROGRAM

A DLI Does Not Automatically Mean PGWP Eligibility

Canada maintains a list of Designated Learning Institutions, commonly called DLIs.

However, simply attending a DLI does not automatically make every graduate eligible for a Post-Graduation Work Permit.

Some institutions have only certain programs that are PGWP eligible, while other programs at the same institution may not qualify.

Students should confirm the PGWP eligibility of the specific institution, campus and program before making important study or immigration decisions.

Check the Program, Not Just the School

A school's DLI status alone is not enough. Your specific program must also meet the applicable PGWP requirements.

LANGUAGE REQUIREMENTS

Language Tests Are Now Required for Most PGWP Applicants

Applicants submitting a PGWP application on or after November 1, 2024 generally need to provide approved English or French language test results.

01

Bachelor's Degree

Graduates of bachelor's degree programs generally need at least CLB 7 in English or NCLC 7 in French in all four language abilities.

02

Master's Degree

Master's degree graduates generally need at least CLB 7 or NCLC 7 in reading, writing, listening and speaking.

03

Doctoral Degree

Doctoral degree graduates generally need at least CLB 7 or NCLC 7 in all four language abilities.

04

Other University Programs

Graduates of other university programs generally need CLB 7 or NCLC 7 in all four language abilities.

05

College and Polytechnic Programs

Graduates of eligible college, polytechnic and other non-university programs generally need at least CLB 5 or NCLC 5 in all four language abilities.

06

Flight School Exception

Graduates of qualifying PGWP-eligible flight schools may be exempt from the language requirement.

FIELD OF STUDY

Does Your Program Need an Eligible CIP Code?

Some PGWP applicants must now graduate from an eligible field of study connected to occupations identified as important to Canada's labour market.

The requirement is based on the Classification of Instructional Programs, commonly referred to as the program's CIP code.

Whether this requirement applies depends on the type of credential and when the applicant submitted their study permit application.

Your CIP Code Can Determine Eligibility

For certain non-degree programs, choosing a program that is not in an eligible field of study can affect future PGWP eligibility.

FIELD OF STUDY RULES

Who Must Meet the Field of Study Requirement?

The field of study requirement does not apply equally to all graduates.

01

Bachelor's Degrees

Graduates with an eligible bachelor's degree do not have a PGWP field of study requirement.

02

Master's Degrees

Graduates with an eligible master's degree do not have a PGWP field of study requirement.

03

Doctoral Degrees

Graduates with an eligible doctoral degree do not have a PGWP field of study requirement.

04

Other University Programs

Graduates from other university programs may need to complete an eligible field of study if their study permit application was submitted on or after November 1, 2024.

05

College or Non-University Programs

Graduates of college, polytechnic and other non-university programs may need an eligible field of study when their study permit application was submitted on or after November 1, 2024.

06

Earlier Study Permit Applications

Applicants who submitted their study permit application before November 1, 2024 generally do not need to meet the PGWP field of study requirement.

ELIGIBLE FIELDS

Current PGWP Field of Study Categories

Where the field of study requirement applies, IRCC maintains a list of eligible six-digit CIP codes within designated categories.

✓

Agriculture and Agri-Food

Certain agricultural, food production and related programs are included.

✓

Education

Certain education and teaching-related programs may qualify.

✓

Health Care and Social Services

Eligible health care and social service programs are included.

✓

STEM

Certain science, technology, engineering and mathematics programs are included.

✓

Trades

A range of skilled trade programs may satisfy the field of study requirement.

✓

Transport

Certain transportation-related programs may also be eligible.

IMPORTANT CIP INFORMATION

The Program Name Alone Is Not Enough

PGWP field of study eligibility is determined using the program's six-digit CIP code, not simply the marketing name that appears on a college or university website.

Two programs with similar names may have different CIP codes and therefore different PGWP implications.

Eligible fields of study can also be updated by the federal government. Students should review the applicable rules based on their study permit application date and program.

Confirm Before Enrolling

If PGWP eligibility is important to your immigration plan, confirm your program's exact CIP code and eligibility before committing to the program.

PERMIT LENGTH

How Long Can a PGWP Be Valid?

PGWP validity depends primarily on the type and length of the completed program, subject to passport validity and other eligibility rules.

01

Program Under 2 Years

For most eligible programs that are at least eight months but less than two years, IRCC may issue a PGWP for up to approximately the same length as the study program.

02

Program 2 Years or Longer

Graduates of eligible programs that are two years or longer may receive a PGWP valid for up to three years.

03

Master's Degree

Eligible master's degree graduates may qualify for a three-year PGWP even when the master's program is less than two years, provided the program is at least eight months long and all other eligibility requirements are met.

04

Multiple Programs

In some situations, the length of two eligible programs may be combined when determining PGWP validity if each program meets the applicable requirements.

MASTER'S GRADUATES

A Shorter Master's Degree Can Still Lead to a 3-Year PGWP

Eligible graduates of master's degree programs have a special PGWP duration rule.

A qualifying master's degree that is at least eight months in duration may lead to a three-year PGWP even if the academic program itself is less than two years long.

This special rule applies to master's degree programs and should not automatically be applied to certificates, graduate certificates or diplomas of a similar duration.

1-Year Master's ≠ 1-Year PGWP

An eligible master's degree of at least eight months may qualify for a PGWP of up to three years.

MULTIPLE PROGRAMS

Can Two Programs Be Combined for PGWP Length?

In certain cases, graduates who complete more than one eligible Canadian program may be able to combine the lengths of the programs when IRCC determines the duration of the PGWP.

Generally, each program used in the calculation must independently meet the applicable PGWP requirements, including the minimum program length.

Where different language or field of study requirements apply to the programs, the applicable eligibility requirements must also be satisfied.

Both Programs Must Be Eligible

Completing two programs does not automatically produce a longer PGWP. Each program should be reviewed before relying on combined program length.

ONE-TIME OPPORTUNITY

You Generally Cannot Receive a Second PGWP

A Post-Graduation Work Permit is generally available only once.

If you previously received a PGWP after completing an earlier program of study, completing another Canadian program does not normally make you eligible for a new PGWP.

For this reason, the timing of a PGWP application can have significant consequences for students who are considering additional studies in Canada.

If you intend to complete another Canadian program, consider how applying for your PGWP now may affect your future immigration strategy.

APPLICATION DEADLINE

You Generally Have 180 Days to Apply

Eligible graduates generally have up to 180 days after their school confirms that they have completed their program to submit a PGWP application.

The completion date may be established through documents such as a completion letter, final transcript or other official confirmation from the educational institution.

Your study permit must also have been valid at some point during the 180-day period after you completed your program.

Do Not Wait for Convocation

The PGWP timeline is connected to confirmation of program completion, not necessarily your graduation ceremony or convocation date.

STATUS IN CANADA

What If Your Study Permit Expires?

Your immigration status at the time of application can affect how you apply and whether restoration is required.

01

Valid Study Permit

Many graduates apply while they still hold valid temporary resident status in Canada.

02

Changed to Visitor

A graduate who has changed their status to visitor may still be able to apply for a PGWP within the applicable 180-day period.

03

Lost Student Status

In certain situations, a graduate whose study permit expired may need to restore their status as a student when applying for the PGWP.

WORKING AFTER GRADUATION

Can You Work While Waiting for Your PGWP?

Some graduates who apply for a PGWP from inside Canada may be authorized to work full-time while IRCC processes their application.

This is not automatic for every applicant. Eligibility depends on the person's status and whether they met the applicable requirements when the PGWP application was submitted.

Graduates should confirm their work authorization before beginning or continuing employment.

Confirm Before You Start Working

Filing a PGWP application does not give every applicant automatic authorization to work. Your individual status and application history should be reviewed.

PASSPORT VALIDITY

Your Passport Can Shorten Your PGWP

A PGWP cannot normally be issued beyond the validity of your passport.

If you are otherwise eligible for a longer PGWP but your passport expires earlier, IRCC may issue the work permit only until the passport expiry date.

In qualifying situations, you may later be able to apply for the remaining period after obtaining a new passport.

Check Your Passport Before Applying

If possible, make sure your passport is valid for the full period of PGWP eligibility before submitting your application.

MEDICAL EXAM

Some Jobs May Require an Immigration Medical Exam

Certain occupations involving health care, children or other protected settings may require an immigration medical examination.

Without an appropriate medical examination, a PGWP may contain restrictions preventing the holder from working in certain occupations.

Graduates planning to work in occupations such as health care or child care should consider whether an upfront medical examination may be appropriate.

AFTER GRADUATION

How a PGWP Can Fit Into Your Permanent Residence Plan

For many international graduates, the PGWP is an important bridge between Canadian education and future permanent residence.

Canadian work experience gained while holding a PGWP may help an applicant qualify for programs such as the Canadian Experience Class or certain Provincial Nominee Programs.

However, simply working in Canada does not guarantee permanent residence. The occupation, TEER category, language results, CRS score, provincial eligibility and other factors can affect the available pathway.

Plan Beyond the PGWP

Your PGWP has a limited validity period. Building a permanent residence strategy early can help you use that time effectively.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

PGWP Rules Have Changed Significantly

PGWP eligibility rules have changed substantially in recent years, including new language requirements and field of study requirements.

Advice that applied to students who began their studies several years ago may not apply to a student beginning the same program today.

Your study permit application date, credential, program CIP code, institution, study history and graduation date should all be considered before determining eligibility.
